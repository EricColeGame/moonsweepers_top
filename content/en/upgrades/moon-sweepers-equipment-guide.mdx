## How equipment upgrades work

A toothbrush is a slow way to clean the Moon. In *Moon Sweepers*, equipment upgrades matter because they help turn collected lunar dust into a larger cleanup operation. The [official Moon Sweepers Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5335510/Moon_Sweepers/) confirms the basic loop: collect dust, deliver it to a depot, launch a shipment to get paid, then spend the earnings on better tools or increased carrying capacity.

The store description does not provide a complete equipment list, upgrade prices, or a definitive best purchase order. This Moon Sweepers equipment guide focuses on what is confirmed and how to make a practical choice between the upgrades described.

## Earn money for your first upgrades

You start with a toothbrush. To fund better equipment, follow the loop described by the developer:

1. Clean dusty terrain and collect lunar dust.
2. Fill your storage and deliver the dust to a depot.
3. Launch a shipment to receive payment.
4. Spend the earnings on cleaning tools or carrying capacity.

The key distinction is that delivering dust and launching a shipment are part of getting paid. The Steam description does not specify how much any shipment earns or how many shipments an upgrade requires.

## Choose between tools and carrying capacity

Better cleaning tools and increased carrying capacity address different parts of the same job. If cleaning is what slows you down, look at tool upgrades. If you regularly fill your storage before finishing the area you are working on, consider carrying capacity so you can collect more between deliveries.

That is a decision framework, not a confirmed upgrade ranking: the available first-party description does not give tool stats or capacity amounts. Check the in-game options before spending, and choose the improvement that addresses your current bottleneck.

## Plan for depots and cleaning vehicles

As your operation grows, *Moon Sweepers* lets you place depots near your work and put cleaning vehicles to use. A nearby depot can make the collect-and-deliver loop more convenient; vehicles are part of the later cleanup operation described on Steam. The source does not identify vehicle models, unlock requirements, or costs, so there is no verified vehicle purchase order to recommend.

In online co-op, lobbies support up to 16 players. Your crew can spread out to cover more ground or work on the same area together. Coordinate where you are cleaning and delivering dust, but check the game itself for any equipment-sharing or upgrade rules—the Steam description does not specify them.
