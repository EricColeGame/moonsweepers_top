## What Is Moon Sweepers?

Developed and published by DragDrop Games, Moon Sweepers launched on **October 23, 2026**. The premise is absurd in the best way: the entire Moon is covered in dust, the fictional Lunar Surface Department has dispatched you and your crew to clean it up — and the budget only covered a single toothbrush.

From there, it's an incremental, co-op cleaning sim. You collect lunar dust, haul it to a depot, launch shipments to get paid, and reinvest your earnings into better brushes, bigger carrying capacity, depots placed closer to your work, and eventually full cleaning vehicles. The game is tagged as an indie, automation, base building, incremental co-op job simulator with open-world exploration elements.
