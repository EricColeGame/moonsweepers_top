## Deliver dust and buy upgrades

Take collected dust to a depot and launch a shipment to get paid. Use those earnings to buy better cleaning tools and increase your carrying capacity. That establishes the core loop:

- Clean terrain and collect lunar dust.
- Deliver the dust to a depot and ship it.
- Spend the earnings on tools or storage upgrades.
- Return to cleaning with improved equipment.

As your operation grows, the game also lets you place depots near your work and use cleaning vehicles. The source does not confirm when vehicles become available or which purchases are most efficient, so treat those as later options rather than a fixed upgrade order.
