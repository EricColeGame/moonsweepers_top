## How moon cleaning works

Your first cleaning tool in *Moon Sweepers* is a toothbrush, but the job spans the lunar surface. Cleaning matters because the dust you collect funds better equipment and a larger operation. According to the [official Moon Sweepers Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5335510/Moon_Sweepers/), the basic loop is to clean dusty terrain, collect lunar dust, ship it for payment, and reinvest the earnings.

The terrain visibly changes as you clean it. You can also track your progress across the Moon, though the Steam description does not specify the controls or a particular cleaning technique.

## How to turn dust into earnings

Collecting dust alone does not complete the payment loop. The developer describes these steps:

1. Fill your storage with lunar dust as you clean.
2. Deliver the dust to a depot.
3. Launch a shipment to get paid.

The Steam description does not give shipment values or timing, so there is no verified best amount to collect before making a delivery.

## What cleaning upgrades do

You can spend your earnings on better cleaning tools and increased carrying capacity. As your operation grows, you can place depots near your work areas and put cleaning vehicles to use.

Those options offer a practical direction for progression: improve your equipment, make room for more dust between deliveries, and position depots closer to where you are cleaning. The official description does not provide a tool list, upgrade costs, or a required purchase order.

## Cleaning the Moon with friends

*Moon Sweepers* supports online co-op lobbies of up to 16 players. Your crew can spread out to cover different areas or clean the same area together. Whichever approach you choose, dust still needs to reach a depot and be shipped before it earns money.

## FAQ

### How do you clean the Moon in Moon Sweepers?

You start with a toothbrush and clean dusty lunar terrain while collecting dust. The developer says each pass visibly changes the terrain, but does not specify detailed cleaning controls in the Steam description.

### How do you sell lunar dust?

Deliver collected dust to a depot and launch a shipment to receive payment.

### Can you upgrade your cleaning equipment?

Yes. Earnings can buy better tools and increased carrying capacity. Cleaning vehicles become available as the operation grows, but the official description does not detail their unlock requirements.

### Can friends help with moon cleaning?

Yes. Online co-op lobbies support up to 16 players, who can work together in one area or spread out across the lunar surface.
