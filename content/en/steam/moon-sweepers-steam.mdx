## What Is Moon Sweepers?

Moon Sweepers is a co-op cleaning and incremental simulation game from developer and publisher DragDrop Games, released on Steam on October 23, 2026. The premise is deliberately absurd: the entire Moon is covered in dust, the fictional Lunar Surface Department (LSD) has dispatched your crew to clean it, and the mission budget only covered a single toothbrush.

From that humble starting point, you collect lunar dust, sell it, and reinvest the earnings into better tools, expanded carrying capacity, and eventually cleaning vehicles. The game sits comfortably in the "job simulator turned tycoon" space, with user tags like Automation, Base Building, Incremental, Open World Simulation, and Comedy. You can find it on its [official Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5335510/Moon_Sweepers/).

## How the Co-op and Gameplay Loop Works

Moon Sweepers supports **online co-op lobbies of up to 16 players**, and you can also play solo. The core loop breaks down into three phases:

1. **Collect lunar dust** — spread out across the surface with your crew, or tackle the same area together.
2. **Deliver and ship** — fill your storage, haul it to a depot, and launch shipments to get paid.
3. **Upgrade and expand** — spend earnings on better cleaning tools and bigger carrying capacity, place depots closer to your work zone, and unlock cleaning vehicles as the operation scales.

The game leans into the comedy of a massively underfunded space program, and the store description openly jokes that whether your friends actually work is "another matter." Progress is visible and satisfying: dusty terrain turns clean with every pass, and you can track how much of the lunar surface your crew has restored.

## Features, Accessibility, and Platform Support

Moon Sweepers ships with single-player and online co-op modes, 33 Steam Achievements, Steam Cloud saves, and Family Sharing. Accessibility options include custom volume controls, stereo sound, no quick-time events, and play-at-your-own-pace support. Controllers from both Xbox and PlayStation are supported via Steam Input.

The game offers interface, full audio, and subtitles in English, with interface localization across 18 additional languages including French, German, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Russian, and both Simplified and Traditional Chinese.

One caveat: **Steam Deck compatibility is currently listed as Unknown**, so handheld players should temper expectations or wait for verified status.
