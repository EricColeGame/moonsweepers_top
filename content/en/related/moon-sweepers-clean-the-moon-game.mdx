Cleaning the entire Moon sounds like a big job—especially when you start with a toothbrush. In *Moon Sweepers*, cleaning is how you collect lunar dust, earn money, and grow your operation. If you’re looking for the Moon Sweepers clean the moon game, here’s what its official Steam listing confirms about the gameplay loop.

## How Do You Clean the Moon in Moon Sweepers?

You begin with a toothbrush and clear dusty lunar terrain. The ground visibly changes as you clean it, and you can track your progress across the surface.

The premise is deliberately simple, but cleaning feeds into a larger cycle: collect dust, deliver it, get paid, and improve your equipment so you can take on more work.

## What Do You Do With Lunar Dust?

According to the [official Moon Sweepers Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5335510/Moon_Sweepers/), the collection-and-upgrade loop works like this:

1. Clean terrain and fill your storage with lunar dust.
2. Deliver the dust to a depot and launch a shipment to get paid.
3. Spend your earnings on better cleaning tools and greater carrying capacity.

As your operation grows, you can place depots near your work and use cleaning vehicles. The listing does not specify an optimal upgrade order, so there’s no verified reason to prioritize a particular purchase.

## Can You Play Solo or With Friends?

*Moon Sweepers* lists both single-player and online co-op. Co-op lobbies support up to 16 players, who can spread out across the lunar surface or work on the same area together.

That flexibility is part of the appeal: you can approach the cleanup alone or make it a shared project. The Steam listing confirms the player limit, but it does not establish a best team size or a required way to divide the work.

## Where Can You Play Moon Sweepers?

DragDrop Games developed and published *Moon Sweepers*, which was released on Steam on October 23, 2026. Its listed minimum PC requirements include Windows 10, 8 GB of RAM, and 2 GB of available storage. Steam Deck compatibility is marked **Unknown**, so the listing does not confirm how well it plays there.
