## What does the official page say about gameplay?

DragDrop Games describes a lunar cleanup game that starts with a toothbrush. Players collect dust, deliver it to a depot, and ship it to earn money for better cleaning tools and greater carrying capacity. The Steam description also mentions placing depots near work areas and using cleaning vehicles as the operation grows.

The page lists both single-player and online co-op, with lobbies of up to 16 players. These are **developer-described features**, not observations verified from watching the trailer.
