## Moon Sweepers Player Reviews: The Verdict So Far

Moon Sweepers has landed well with its audience. As of October 29, 2026, the game holds a **Very Positive** rating on Steam among English-language reviewers, with 80% of 1,444 English user reviews positive. Across all languages, the store page lists 2,708 total reviews, of which 2,133 are positive and 575 negative — an overall picture that trends "Mostly Positive" globally but sits stronger in English and German communities (German reviews are also rated Very Positive, with 214 reviews).

That's a solid launch reception for a $8.99 indie sim from DragDrop Games, which released on October 23, 2026. The game is currently 15% off at $7.64 as part of an introductory offer, which may partly explain the rapid review volume.

## What Moon Sweepers Actually Is

For context behind the reviews: Moon Sweepers is a first-person cleaning and automation sim where the Lunar Surface Department (LSD) sends you and your crew to clean an entire Moon — armed, at first, with nothing but a toothbrush. The core loop per the official [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5335510/Moon_Sweepers/):

- Collect lunar dust and fill your storage
- Deliver it to a depot and launch shipments to get paid
- Spend earnings on better tools and increased carrying capacity
- Place depots near your work and unlock cleaning vehicles as the operation grows

It supports online co-op with up to 16 players, plus single-player, and tags itself with automation, base building, incremental, job simulator, and comedy.

## Who the Reviews Suggest This Game Is For

Based on the store page's own framing and user tags — relaxing, comedy, co-op, incremental — the positive reception appears to center on players who enjoy low-stress, progression-driven cleanup sims in the vein of PowerWash Simulator-style jobs, but with an automation and multiplayer twist. The page's own copy leans into the joke: "Whether everyone actually works is another matter," a nod to the chaos of 16-player co-op lobbies.

If you're deciding whether to buy, the accessibility features are worth noting: it's playable at your own pace, has no quick-time events, custom volume controls, and full controller support for Xbox and PlayStation pads. There are 33 Steam achievements, and Steam Cloud and Family Sharing are supported. Steam Deck compatibility is currently listed as unknown.

## Should You Trust the Very Positive Rating?

Steam review scores are always a snapshot. A few caveats before you jump in:

1. **The game is barely a week old** (as of this writing). Early reviews often reflect the honeymoon phase, and long-term progression pacing hasn't been stress-tested by time yet.
2. **The launch discount may be inflating positivity.** A $7.64 entry price lowers the bar for a thumbs-up.
3. **The developer has disclosed AI-generated content** in the game, which some players weigh heavily — check the Steam disclosure if that matters to you.

With that said, 80% positive across nearly 1,500 English reviews is a strong signal, and the active update cadence suggests the developer is engaged.
