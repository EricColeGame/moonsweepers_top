## How progression works

A toothbrush is only the beginning in Moon Sweepers. Cleaning lunar dust earns you money for better equipment, so your purchases affect how much work you can take on between deliveries. The confirmed loop is straightforward: collect dust, deliver it to a depot, launch a shipment to get paid, and spend the earnings on your operation.

That loop comes from the developer’s [Moon Sweepers Steam description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5335510/Moon_Sweepers/). It confirms the broad progression path, but does not provide an upgrade tree, prices, or a definitive purchase order.

## Which upgrades are confirmed?

The Steam description identifies better cleaning tools, equipment upgrades, and increased carrying capacity as ways to expand your operation. It also says you can place depots near your work and eventually use cleaning vehicles.

These serve different parts of the cleanup cycle:

- **Cleaning tools** help you move beyond the starting toothbrush.
- **Carrying capacity** lets you take on more work between deliveries.
- **Depots** can be placed closer to the area you are cleaning.
- **Cleaning vehicles** become available as the operation grows.

The source does not name specific tool tiers or vehicle models, or state exactly when each option unlocks.

## How should you choose your next purchase?

Use the part of the loop that slows you down as a guide. If cleaning feels like the limiting step, look at the available tool upgrades. If you repeatedly stop work to deliver dust, consider carrying capacity. As your work moves across the lunar surface, consider whether a nearby depot would make deliveries more convenient.

This is a practical way to evaluate the confirmed options, **not a tested fastest-progression route**. Check the choices and costs shown in your game before spending; the available source does not establish which purchase offers the best return.

## Does co-op change the upgrade path?

Moon Sweepers supports online co-op lobbies of up to 16 players. The developer says a crew can spread out or clean the same area together, which may change where you want to work and place depots.

The Steam description does not explain whether players share money, purchases, or upgrade unlocks. Coordinate your cleanup and deliveries with your group, but verify those rules in-game rather than assuming everyone follows one shared upgrade path.

## FAQ

### What are Moon Sweepers progression upgrades?

They are improvements to your cleanup operation. The developer confirms better cleaning tools, equipment upgrades, increased carrying capacity, placeable depots, and cleaning vehicles, without publishing a complete upgrade list in the cited description.

### How do you earn money for upgrades?

Collect lunar dust, deliver it to a depot, and launch a shipment to get paid. You can then spend the earnings on improvements.

### What should I upgrade first?

There is no verified universal first purchase in the available source. Compare the options in your game and choose based on whether cleaning speed or frequent deliveries are holding you back.

### When do cleaning vehicles unlock?

The developer says vehicles become part of the operation as it grows, but the cited description does not give a specific unlock requirement.
