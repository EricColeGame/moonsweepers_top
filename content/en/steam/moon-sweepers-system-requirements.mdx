Moon Sweepers is a friendly game for your hardware. According to the official [Moon Sweepers Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5335510/Moon_Sweepers/), you only need an Intel Core i5-8400 or AMD Ryzen 5 2600, 8 GB of RAM, and a GTX 1650 or RX 570 to run it, with just 2 GB of free storage. Even the recommended specs sit comfortably in mid-range territory, so most gaming PCs built in the last several years can handle this lunar cleanup sim without breaking a sweat.

## Moon Sweepers System Requirements at a Glance

DragDrop Games released Moon Sweepers on October 23, 2026, and the developers list the following PC requirements directly on Steam.

**Minimum requirements:**

- 64-bit processor and operating system
- OS: Windows 10
- CPU: Intel Core i5-8400 / AMD Ryzen 5 2600
- RAM: 8 GB
- GPU: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650 / AMD Radeon RX 570
- DirectX: Version 11
- Network: Broadband internet connection
- Storage: 2 GB available space

**Recommended requirements:**

- 64-bit processor and operating system
- OS: Windows 11
- CPU: Intel Core i5-10400 / AMD Ryzen 5 3600
- RAM: 16 GB
- GPU: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1660 Super / AMD Radeon RX 5600 XT
- DirectX: Version 12
- Network: Broadband internet connection
- Storage: 4 GB available space

## Why the Requirements Are So Modest

For a first-person, open-world cleaning sim, Moon Sweepers keeps its footprint small. The entire install fits in 2 GB, and the recommended tier only doubles that to 4 GB. There's no listed ray tracing, no VR requirement, and no ultra-tier hardware suggested for 4K play. The game is built around incremental progression — sweeping dust, hauling it to depots, and upgrading tools — rather than demanding graphical showcases.

One thing worth noting: because the game supports online co-op lobbies with up to 16 players, a broadband internet connection is listed in both spec tiers. Even if you plan to play single-player, expect the game to assume you're online.

## Can Your PC Run Moon Sweepers?

If your machine can run games like Satisfactory, ASTRONEER, or Oxygen Not Included at reasonable settings, you're well above the bar here. A GTX 1650 is one of the most common entry-level GPUs of the last few years, and Ryzen 5 2600-class CPUs are ubiquitous in budget builds from 2018 onward.

Quick checklist before you buy:

- Confirm you're on a 64-bit version of Windows (the game won't run otherwise)
- Make sure you have at least 2 GB free on your drive
- Update your GPU drivers before your first launch
- Have a stable internet connection ready, especially for co-op sessions

## Steam Deck and Controller Support

The Steam page currently lists Moon Sweepers' Steam Deck compatibility as "Unknown," so Valve hasn't officially verified it yet. That said, the game does support Xbox and PlayStation controllers through Steam Input, which gives you flexibility if you prefer couch-style play on a desktop setup.

On the accessibility front, the developers list four features, including custom volume controls, stereo sound support, no quick-time events, and the ability to play at your own pace. That last one fits the game's relaxed, incremental loop nicely.

## Is Moon Sweepers Worth the Download Size?

At 2 GB, Moon Sweepers costs you less drive space than most game patches. It launched with "Very Positive" English reviews (80% of 1,444 reviews at the time of writing), 33 Steam achievements, Steam Cloud saves, and Family Sharing support. The premise is simple but sticky: the Lunar Surface Department sent you to clean the Moon, gave you a toothbrush, and the rest of the operation is up to you and up to 15 friends. If you've got an aging GPU and a taste for job sims, the low system requirements make this an easy impulse buy.

## FAQ

### What are the minimum system requirements for Moon Sweepers?

You need a 64-bit Windows 10 PC with an Intel Core i5-8400 or AMD Ryzen 5 2600, 8 GB of RAM, a GTX 1650 or RX 570, DirectX 11, a broadband connection, and 2 GB of free storage.

### Can Moon Sweepers run on a low-end PC?

Yes, likely. The minimum specs target entry-level hardware from around 2018, and the game's small 2 GB install size reflects its modest demands. If your PC runs other modern indie sims, it should handle Moon Sweepers.

### Does Moon Sweepers work on Steam Deck?

Not officially, at least not yet. The Steam listing shows Steam Deck compatibility as "Unknown." The game does support controllers via Steam Input, but Valve hasn't verified Deck performance.

### Do I need internet to play Moon Sweepers?

A broadband internet connection is listed in both the minimum and recommended requirements, and the game features online co-op for up to 16 players. Official single-player offline play isn't specified beyond the single-player feature tag, so assume a connection is expected.
