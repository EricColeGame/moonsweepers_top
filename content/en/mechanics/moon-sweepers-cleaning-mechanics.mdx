Moon Sweepers is built around one deceptively simple loop: the entire Moon is covered in dust, and it's your job to clean it. You start with nothing but a toothbrush, collect lunar dust by hand, sell it for cash, and reinvest those earnings into better tools, bigger storage, and eventually full cleaning vehicles. The cleaning mechanics are the heart of the game — everything else, from co-op play to base building, hangs off that core cycle.

Here's how the system works, based on the game's official Steam page (developer: DragDrop Games, released October 23, 2026).

## The Core Cleaning Loop

The gameplay revolves around a four-step cycle that repeats as your operation grows:

1. **Collect lunar dust** — you physically clean dusty terrain, and every pass visibly turns dirty ground clean.
2. **Fill your storage** — collected dust goes into your personal carrying capacity, which limits how much you can haul before a delivery.
3. **Deliver to a depot** — drop off your dust at a depot and launch shipments to get paid.
4. **Upgrade** — spend your earnings on new cleaning tools and increased carrying capacity so you can take on bigger jobs between deliveries.

The loop is deliberately incremental. The game's own description calls it "one painfully small brushstroke at a time," and early game progress is intentionally slow until your upgrades start compounding.

## Tools, Upgrades, and Cleaning Vehicles

Your starting toothbrush is the joke the whole game is built on. The Lunar Surface Department (LSD) sent you to clean the Moon, but the budget only covered that single brush. From there, progression is about replacing that toothbrush with better cleaning tools and unlocking cleaning vehicles as your operation scales.

Steam achievements hint at the upgrade path — "One Small Sweep" and "Get A Better Brush" appear among the game's 33 achievements, reinforcing that tool progression is a core milestone system.

## Depots and Shipment Sales

Selling dust is where the money comes from. You fill your storage with lunar dust, deliver it to a depot, and launch shipments to receive payment. Depots aren't fixed at one location either — you can place depots near your work area as your operation grows, cutting down travel time between cleaning zones and delivery points.

This ties the cleaning mechanics directly into the base-building and automation elements the game is tagged with on Steam. Where you position depots shapes how efficient your hauling routes are.

## How Cleaning Works in Co-op

Moon Sweepers supports online co-op lobbies of up to 16 players (it's also playable single-player). The cleaning mechanics scale to the group in two ways:

- **Spread out** — cover more ground across the lunar surface, cleaning different zones simultaneously.
- **Work together** — tackle the same area as a team for faster progress on a single region.

The developers are upfront that there's enough dust to keep everyone busy — the real question, as the Steam page jokes, is whether everyone actually works.

## Tracking Progress Across the Moon

Because the cleaning is persistent and visible, you can track your progress across the lunar surface as terrain turns from dusty to clean. The open-world structure means the whole Moon is your job site, and your visible cleanup progress is the measure of how far your operation has come. Steam Cloud saves and the achievement list (including "Dust Delivery") support the long-haul nature of the grind.

## FAQ

**How do you make money in Moon Sweepers?**
You collect lunar dust while cleaning, fill your storage, deliver it to a depot, and launch shipments in exchange for payment. Earnings fund tool upgrades and carrying capacity.

**What tools do you start with in Moon Sweepers?**
A toothbrush. The game's premise is that the Lunar Surface Department's budget only covered one, and better cleaning tools and vehicles are unlocked through progression.

**Can you play Moon Sweepers with friends?**
Yes — online co-op supports up to 16 players in a lobby, and you can either split up to clean more ground or work the same area together. Single-player is also supported.

**Do cleaning vehicles matter in Moon Sweepers?**
Yes. As your operation grows, you unlock cleaning vehicles and can place depots near your work, turning the early hand-cleaning grind into a larger, more automated cleanup business.
