## What Moon Sweepers Actually Is

Moon Sweepers is a first-person co-op cleaning and incremental sim from developer and publisher DragDrop Games. The premise is deliberately absurd: the Moon is covered in dust, the fictional Lunar Surface Department (LSD) has sent you and your crew to clean it, and your entire starting budget covered exactly one toothbrush.

From there, it's a classic progression loop:

- **Collect lunar dust** by brushing dusty terrain, watching it turn clean with every pass
- **Sell your haul** by delivering it to a depot and launching shipments
- **Upgrade your gear** with better tools and increased carrying capacity
- **Expand the operation** by placing depots near your work area and unlocking cleaning vehicles

The game carries user tags like Automation, Base Building, Incremental, Job Simulator, Open World, and Relaxing — so expect a laid-back grind rather than a tense survival experience.

## Co-op With Up to 16 Players

The headline feature is online co-op supporting **lobbies of up to 16 players**. Your crew can spread out to cover more ground or work the same area together, and the game leans into the comedy of whether your friends actually pull their weight. There's enough dust to keep everyone busy; whether everyone works is another matter entirely.

Moon Sweepers also supports full single-player, so you can build your lunar cleaning empire solo if you prefer. Other listed features include Steam Achievements (33 total), Steam Cloud saves, and Family Sharing. Controller support covers Xbox and PlayStation pads via Steam Input, and the game includes accessibility options like custom volume controls and a play-at-your-own-pace mode with no quick-time events.
