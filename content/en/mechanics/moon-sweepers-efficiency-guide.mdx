## The short answer

Cleaning faster means little if your storage fills up far from a depot. In *Moon Sweepers*, the most useful efficiency plan is to work near a depot, deliver collected lunar dust, launch shipments for payment, and use those earnings to improve your tools or carrying capacity. The developer confirms those mechanics on the [Moon Sweepers Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5335510/Moon_Sweepers/), but does not publish upgrade costs or a proven best purchase order.

## Keep deliveries close to the work

Dust must be delivered to a depot and shipped before it earns money. The game also lets you place depots near your work area. As your cleaning operation expands, consider whether a closer depot would shorten the trip from the terrain you are currently clearing.

You can track cleaning progress across the lunar surface. Use that view to identify where work remains, then plan around both the dusty area and the route back to a depot. The available source does not establish an ideal depot spacing, so treat placement as a practical travel decision rather than a fixed formula.

## Choose upgrades for the bottleneck you notice

Moon Sweepers starts you with a toothbrush and lets you spend earnings on better cleaning tools and increased carrying capacity. Those upgrades address different interruptions:

- **Cleaning feels slow:** Look at better tools when clearing dust is taking most of your time.
- **You stop frequently to deliver dust:** Consider more carrying capacity when storage fills before you have finished a useful stretch of work.

Neither choice is universally best based on the published information. Watch which part of your own collect-deliver-ship cycle keeps stopping progress, and spend your next earnings there. Cleaning vehicles become available as the operation grows, but the source does not specify when to buy one or how it compares with other upgrades.

## Coordinate the crew

Online co-op supports up to 16 players. The developer says crews can spread out across the surface or work together in one area, so agree on which areas people are cleaning before starting a long session. That gives everyone a clear place to work and makes progress easier to follow.

If your crew is deciding between spreading out and concentrating on one patch, consider the distance each player must travel to deliver dust. The published description does not confirm a co-op bonus for either approach; choose the arrangement that fits your current depot locations and the areas left to clean.

## FAQ

### What is the main tip in this Moon Sweepers efficiency guide?

Pay attention to the full cycle: collect dust, deliver it to a depot, launch a shipment, and spend the earnings. Keeping delivery travel manageable and upgrading the part of that cycle that slows you down are practical starting points supported by the game's described mechanics.

### Should I buy a better tool or more carrying capacity first?

There is no verified universal purchase order. A better tool addresses slow cleaning; more capacity addresses frequent storage interruptions. Choose based on which problem you encounter most often.

### Can I place depots near the area I am cleaning?

Yes. The developer says players can place depots near their work. The available description does not specify placement costs or limits.

### Is working together faster than spreading out in co-op?

The developer confirms both approaches are possible, but does not provide a speed comparison. Coordinate around your crew's current work areas and delivery routes rather than assuming one approach is always faster.
