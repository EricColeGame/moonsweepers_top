The Moon Sweepers community is in the middle of a fast-moving conversation between players and developers — and it's worth paying attention to. The game, described on its [official Steam community hub](https://steamcommunity.com/app/5335510) as "an entire Moon to clean" with one toothbrush to start, supports up to 16 players working together to collect and sell lunar dust. At the time of writing, more than 44,000 players were in-game, and the developers have shipped two major patches in a single week. Here's a full rundown of what's happening in the community as of October 29, 2026.

## What Moon Sweepers Is, in Brief

Moon Sweepers is a cooperative cleaning game where you and up to 15 friends sweep an entire Moon for dust, sell it, upgrade your tools, and unlock cleaning vehicles. The Steam listing frames it as turning "a ridiculous job into a thriving operation, if your friends actually help." The community hub currently shows active group chat, global achievement stats, player screenshots, guides, and lively discussion threads.

## Two Patches in One Week: 1.0.4 and 1.0.5

The development team (a two-person studio) has been patching rapidly in response to launch feedback.

### Patch 1.0.4 (October 25)

Highlights from the official patch notes include:

- **J.O.O.N.A.S. improvements:** The cleaning robot now cleans hexes from the outside in, is roughly 2x faster, works even when no players are nearby, and no longer needs to return to a dock.
- **Automatic save backups:** Each save slot keeps up to 5 full backups, taken a minute into each session and then every 30 minutes, with automatic repair for damaged saves.
- **Multiplayer fixes:** Ground synchronization was reworked so everyone sees the same cleaned ground, and any crewmate can place splitters and mergers on shared belts.
- **Steam Deck/Linux and AMD fixes:** Startup issues with older Proton versions were addressed, and DirectX 11 is now the default on Windows/AMD to prevent GPU crashes (DX12 available via the `-force-d3d12` launch option).

Note: saves made in 1.0.4 cannot be opened in older versions of the game.

### Patch 1.0.5 (October 28)

The most recent update focuses on building flexibility and stability:

- Build on, under, and beside foundations, with full refunds on removed pieces instead of 75%.
- Smarter J.O.O.N.A.S. capsule delivery that spreads capsules across the emptiest catchers.
- A fix for the game freezing roughly half a minute after loading some worlds.
- The ground capsule limit was raised from 10,000 to 90,000 per world.
- New options including a public game warning, chat voice slider, and vehicle camera toggle.

## The Developers' Open Letter: "Thank You & We Are Sorry"

On October 24, the two-person team posted a candid message to the community acknowledging that the launch "hasn't been perfect." They apologized for crashes, performance issues, multiplayer bugs, automation gaps, and progression balancing concerns, and confirmed they'd already shipped fixes including shared crew upgrades, host kick/ban controls, and save file protection. They also pointed to J.O.O.N.A.S. units stopping when players move away and remaining performance problems as their highest priorities, and invited players to report bugs in the official Discord.

That message drew significant engagement — over 1,700 reactions and more than 100 comments on the hub — a sign of how invested the community already is.

## What the Community Is Saying

Player feedback spans the full spectrum:

- **End-game concerns:** One player with 37+ hours reported hitting a throughput bottleneck around 16% cleaned, arguing there isn't much late game after running out of upgrades and requesting deeper automation paths.
- **Performance at scale:** A speedrun-oriented player described frame drops from 144 FPS down to single digits as their base and robot count grew, though they still called the game fun.
- **Automation critics:** Another reviewer felt the incremental and base-building elements were too shallow to sustain a long run, praising the concept but calling the game unfinished.
- **Achievement hunting:** Discussion threads include tips for the 10,000-deposit achievement "Do You Live Here?", with players noting that conveyor-delivered capsules don't count toward progress.
- **Community creativity:** The hub is active with guides like "Most efficient factory layout" and "End Game Ratio for Catchers," plus screenshots of players at 50% cleaned with 500+ J.O.O.N.A.S. units.

## How to Get Involved

If you want to join the Moon Sweepers community, your options are straightforward: participate in discussions on the [Steam community hub](https://steamcommunity.com/app/5335510), report bugs in the developers' Discord, share screenshots and guides, and keep an eye on the news feed — patches have been arriving within days of each other. ## FAQ

**Is Moon Sweepers multiplayer?**
Yes. It supports up to 16 players co-op, with crew upgrades now shared across the whole crew after recent patches.

**What is J.O.O.N.A.S. in Moon Sweepers?**
J.O.O.N.A.S. is the in-game cleaning robot that automatically sweeps lunar hexes and launches dust capsules to your capsule catchers. Patches 1.0.4 and 1.0.5 significantly improved its speed, navigation, and capsule delivery logic.

**How active is the Moon Sweepers community?**
Very active for a new launch: over 44,000 players were in-game as of October 29, 2026, with daily discussions, guides, and rapid developer responses on the Steam hub.

**Are old saves compatible after the patches?**
No — the developers explicitly noted that saves made in patch 1.0.4 cannot be opened in older versions of the game. Automatic backups were also added to protect your worlds going forward.
