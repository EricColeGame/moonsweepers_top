Dust collection is the entire economy of Moon Sweepers. Released October 23, 2026 by DragDrop Games, the co-op cleaning sim hands you an entire Moon covered in dust and a budget that apparently only covered a toothbrush. Everything you earn comes from sweeping that dust up, hauling it to a depot, and shipping it out for cash — so understanding the loop is the difference between a thriving lunar operation and a very clean fingernail.

## The Core Dust Collection Loop

According to the game's official Steam page, the loop breaks down into four repeatable steps:

- **Sweep.** You start with a painfully small toothbrush and scrub dusty terrain until it turns clean with every pass.
- **Store.** Collected lunar dust fills your personal storage, which caps how much you can gather before you need to unload.
- **Deliver.** Carry your full load to a depot and launch shipments to get paid.
- **Upgrade.** Spend your earnings on better cleaning tools and increased carrying capacity so each trip before a delivery covers more ground.

That last step is what turns the game from a joke job into an incremental, automation-flavored operation. Bigger capacity means longer stretches of sweeping between deliveries, which is where the "growing business" fantasy kicks in.

## Depots and Progress Tracking

Placement matters. The Steam description specifically calls out the ability to place depots near your work, which cuts down the dead time between a full storage load and getting paid. You can also track your progress across the lunar surface, watching previously dusty terrain stay clean — a satisfying visual ledger of how much dust you've actually moved.

## Scaling Up with Vehicles

As your operation grows, you unlock cleaning vehicles to replace — or at least supplement — manual brushing. The page doesn't detail the vehicle roster or exact unlock conditions, but vehicles are positioned as the late-game answer to the sheer scale of "an entire Moon to clean." Combined with placed depots, they're the backbone of the automation tag the game carries on Steam.

## Co-op: Dust Collection With (Or Without) Your Friends

Moon Sweepers supports online co-op lobbies of up to 16 players, plus full single-player. The design intent is flexible: spread out to cover more territory, or gang up on the same area together. There's enough dust to keep everyone busy. Whether everyone actually works, as the developers themselves put it, is another matter — the game leans into that comedy, so don't expect your lobbymates to split the workload evenly.

## FAQ

**How does dust collection work in Moon Sweepers?**
You sweep dusty terrain to fill your storage, deliver the lunar dust to a depot, launch shipments to get paid, and reinvest in better tools and carrying capacity to collect more per trip.

**How many players can collect dust together in co-op?**
Online co-op supports up to 16 players in a single lobby. You can split up to cover more ground or work the same area together.

**What tools do you start with for collecting dust?**
You start with a toothbrush — the joke premise of the game. Upgrades unlock better cleaning tools and eventually cleaning vehicles as your operation grows.

**What's the fastest way to make money from dust?**
Based on the official description: upgrade carrying capacity early so each depot run moves more dust, and place depots near your work area to cut travel time between sweeps and deliveries.
