## What should you upgrade first in Moon Sweepers?

Your first purchase should address the part of the cleanup cycle that slows you down most. If collecting dust takes longer than delivering it, prioritize a better cleaning tool. If you keep filling storage before you can finish an area, prioritize carrying capacity instead. Save cleaning vehicles for when your operation has grown enough to use them.

That is a practical recommendation, not a confirmed optimal build. The [official Moon Sweepers Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5335510/Moon_Sweepers/) confirms these upgrade categories and the dust-shipment loop, but does not provide upgrade prices or performance figures to establish a universal purchase order.

## Start with the collection bottleneck

Moon Sweepers begins with a toothbrush. You collect lunar dust, bring it to a depot, launch a shipment, and spend the earnings on equipment. Better cleaning tools are therefore a sensible early priority when gathering enough dust for a shipment feels slow.

Watch what happens during a typical run. If you have room to carry more dust but spend most of your time cleaning, improving the tool addresses the delay you can actually observe. There is no verified tool-by-tool ranking in the available source, so compare the options shown in your game before spending.

## Increase capacity when deliveries interrupt your work

The Steam description explicitly identifies increased carrying capacity as an upgrade. Consider it when storage fills so often that you must stop cleaning and return to a depot before making meaningful progress in an area.

Capacity does not replace cleaning speed. If your storage rarely fills, a larger load may offer little immediate benefit. The useful question is whether your next upgrade would let you clean more between shipments or simply leave you with unused space.

## Place depots and consider vehicles as you expand

The developer says you can place depots near your work and use cleaning vehicles as the operation grows. A nearby depot may be worth prioritizing when travel to deliver dust becomes the main interruption. That is a placement decision as much as a spending decision: assess where you are working before committing resources.

Vehicles fit a later, larger-scale cleanup plan, but the source does not specify their costs, unlock requirements, or efficiency. Do not assume a vehicle is automatically a better purchase than improving the tool, capacity, or depot access your current work depends on.

## Adjust priorities for your crew

Moon Sweepers supports solo play and online co-op with up to 16 players. In a group, players can spread out or work on the same area, so upgrade needs may differ across the crew. Someone cleaning far from a depot might feel the delivery burden more than someone working nearby.

Use the same check whether you play alone or together: identify whether collecting, carrying, or delivering dust is holding up the next shipment, then spend toward that constraint. The available first-party description does not establish a separate best upgrade path for co-op.

## FAQ

### What are the best Moon Sweepers upgrade priorities for beginners?

Start by comparing cleaning speed with how often your storage fills. Favor a better tool if collection is slow; favor carrying capacity if frequent deliveries interrupt cleaning. Reassess depot access as you move farther across the Moon.

### Should I buy a better tool or more carrying capacity first?

Buy for the bottleneck you encounter. The official description confirms both options, but it does not provide enough performance or cost data to declare one the best first purchase for every player.

### When should I get a cleaning vehicle?

Consider one as your cleanup operation expands. Vehicles are confirmed as part of the game, but their unlock requirements, costs, and relative value are not established by the available source.

### Can depots help with upgrade priorities?

Yes. Moon Sweepers lets you place depots near your work. If delivery travel is slowing your shipments, improving depot access may matter more immediately than buying another collection upgrade.
