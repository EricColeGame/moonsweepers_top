## The progression loop

Starting with a toothbrush makes cleaning the Moon a big job. The key to progressing in *Moon Sweepers* is turning collected lunar dust into earnings you can spend on better equipment. According to the [official Moon Sweepers Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5335510/Moon_Sweepers/), the loop is to collect dust, fill your storage, deliver it to a depot, launch a shipment, and use the payment to expand your cleanup operation.

That source describes the broad path, but it does not provide upgrade costs, unlock requirements, or a definitive purchase order. This Moon Sweepers progression guide sticks to what is confirmed and offers practical choices within those limits.

## Start by collecting and shipping dust

Clean terrain to gather lunar dust until your storage is full, then take it to a depot and launch a shipment to get paid. Delivering dust matters: collecting it alone is not the full earnings loop described by the developer.

As you work, keep track of which terrain still needs cleaning. The Steam description confirms that you can track progress across the lunar surface, though it does not specify a particular route or area to tackle first.

## Spend earnings on the bottleneck you face

The developer confirms that earnings can go toward better cleaning tools and increased carrying capacity. Choose based on what is slowing your next shipment:

- **Cleaning feels slow:** Look at available tool upgrades.
- **Storage fills too quickly:** Consider increased carrying capacity so you can collect more between deliveries.

These are decision guidelines, not a verified ranking of upgrades. The available source does not establish which purchase pays for itself fastest or whether one upgrade must be bought before another.

## Expand the operation with depots, vehicles, and co-op

As your work spreads across the Moon, the game lets you place depots near the area you are cleaning and put cleaning vehicles to use. The developer does not specify when vehicles unlock or what they cost, so treat them as a later progression goal rather than a guaranteed next purchase.

You can also play solo or join an online co-op lobby of up to 16 players. In a group, you can spread out to cover more terrain or work on the same area together. Coordinate where people are cleaning and which depot they are using; no specific team size or division of labor is established as optimal.

## FAQ

### What is the basic Moon Sweepers progression guide?

Collect lunar dust, bring it to a depot, launch a shipment for payment, and spend the earnings on equipment such as better tools or increased carrying capacity. Depots and cleaning vehicles can support a larger operation as you progress.

### Should I upgrade tools or carrying capacity first?

The official description does not give a universal best choice. Check whether cleaning speed or frequent storage limits are holding you back, then choose an available upgrade that addresses that problem.

### How do I unlock cleaning vehicles?

The developer confirms that cleaning vehicles are part of the game but does not state their unlock requirements in the available source. Check the in-game progression options for the conditions in your current game.
