## Your first hour in Moon Sweepers, answered

The entire first hour of Moon Sweepers boils down to one loop: start with the toothbrush, fill a container with lunar dust, drop it at the depot, and launch it for pay. That is not an oversimplification — global achievement rates checked on October 24 and 27, 2026 show that essentially every new player completes the same opening milestones. This guide walks through that loop in order, what to buy first, and a few player-reported habits that save time.

## The core loop, step by step

The setup is deliberately absurd: the Lunar Surface Department sends you to a dusty Moon armed with nothing but a toothbrush. You sweep lunar dust, pack it, carry it to a depot, and launch a shipment for payment. That money funds a better brush, more carrying capacity, and eventually a vehicle.

The opening milestones, matched to the game's achievements, play out like this:

- Clean a patch of dust (One Small Sweep)
- Deposit dust at the depot (Dust Delivery)
- Launch a full container (Return to Sender)
- Collect 10 kg of dust (Dust Bunny)
- Upgrade your brush (Get A Better Brush)
- Finish the first region (That's Going to Take a While)

Nearly all of these sat at a 100% global completion rate in late October 2026, with the first-region milestone at 99.9%. In other words, if you follow the tutorial flow, you are doing exactly what the game intends.

## What to buy with your first payout

There is no officially published price for the first depot or the first shipment, so plan around relative priorities rather than exact numbers. The safest first purchase is the brush upgrade — the achievement data treats it as something every player does. After that, carrying capacity and eventually a vehicle are your progression path.

## Player-reported tips for the early game

A few habits come from community discussion rather than official documentation, so treat them as unconfirmed until you verify them in-game:

- Players report that dust capsules hold 1 kg, while the brush capacity grows from 2 kg to 4 kg as you upgrade.
- One reply on the dust-capacity thread claims you don't need to manually pull capsules out of the brush — you can walk the brush straight to the intake and dump it. The specific key bind for this has not been confirmed in-game.
- Since patch 1.0.3, demolishing a garage no longer deletes the vehicles inside; they relocate to another garage. That makes early base layout mistakes far less punishing.
- Since patch 1.0.4, right-clicking the Moon map lets you set a waypoint or teleport to a discovered station, and the teleport panel lets you hop between stations you've found.

## What Moon Sweepers is not

Set expectations before you invest a weekend. One 16.9-hour review notes the first two hours feel great, but the automation layer shows its limits after that. Treat the early game as a short, charming co-op toy rather than a full factory builder. If you go in expecting deep production chains, you may bounce off; if you want a relaxed few hours of sweeping with friends, the first stretch delivers.

One more caveat: DragDrop Games has not published an official key binding list. Binds like WASD, E, F, and Ctrl+scroll appear only in player posts, so check the in-game options menu first.

For the official word on the game, including screenshots and store details, see the [official Moon Sweepers Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5335510/Moon_Sweepers/).

## FAQ

### What is the first thing I should do in Moon Sweepers?

Sweep a patch of lunar dust with your starting toothbrush, carry it to the depot, and launch a full container. That loop covers the first several achievements and earns your first payout.

### What should I spend my first payout on?

The brush upgrade. The achievement list indicates virtually every player buys it first, and it directly speeds up the core loop.

### Does deleting a garage destroy my vehicles in Moon Sweepers?

Not since patch 1.0.3. Vehicles inside a demolished garage now relocate to another garage instead of being deleted.

### Is there an official control list for Moon Sweepers?

No. DragDrop Games has not published key binds on an official page. Community posts mention WASD, E, F, and Ctrl+scroll, but you should confirm bindings in the game's own options menu.
