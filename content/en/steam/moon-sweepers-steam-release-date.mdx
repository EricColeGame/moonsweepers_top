## What Kind of Game Is Moon Sweepers?

The premise is delightfully absurd: the Moon is covered in dust, and the fictional Lunar Surface Department (LSD) has dispatched you and your crew to clean it all up. The catch? The budget only covered a toothbrush.

From there, it's a progression loop in the "job simulator" mold:

- **Collect lunar dust** with whatever tool you can afford
- **Sell your haul** by delivering it to a depot and launching shipments
- **Upgrade your tools** and carrying capacity to tackle bigger jobs between deliveries
- **Unlock cleaning vehicles** and place depots near your work area as the operation scales

You start with one painfully small brushstroke at a time and work your way up to running a full lunar cleaning business. The game is tagged as an indie automation, base building, incremental, and open-world simulation title, with a relaxing, comedic first-person tone.

## Co-op With Up to 16 Players

One of Moon Sweepers' biggest draws is its online co-op. You can team up with up to 16 players in a single lobby. Your crew can split up to cover more ground or work the same area together — there's plenty of dust to go around, though the game itself jokes about whether everyone will actually pitch in.

The game supports both single-player and online co-op, with Steam Achievements (33 total), Steam Cloud saves, and Family Sharing. Controller support covers Xbox and PlayStation controllers via Steam Input.
