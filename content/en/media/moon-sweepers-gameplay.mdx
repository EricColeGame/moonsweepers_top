## What is Moon Sweepers gameplay like?

An entire Moon needs cleaning, and your crew starts with a toothbrush. In *Moon Sweepers*, the core loop is to clean dusty terrain, collect lunar dust, ship it for payment, and spend the earnings on better equipment. That matters because each upgrade helps your cleanup operation take on more ground between deliveries, whether you play alone or with friends.

The [official Moon Sweepers Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5335510/Moon_Sweepers/) describes a game built around visible cleaning progress: dusty ground changes as you work across it.

## How the cleanup loop works

The developer describes a straightforward cycle:

1. Clean terrain and collect lunar dust in your storage.
2. Deliver the dust to a depot and launch a shipment to get paid.
3. Use the earnings to improve your tools or carrying capacity.
4. Continue cleaning with the upgraded equipment.

You can place depots near the area where you’re working and track your progress across the lunar surface. The Steam description does not specify shipment values or exact upgrade costs, so there is no verified “best” purchase order to recommend.

## From a toothbrush to cleaning vehicles

The toothbrush is your starting point, not the extent of your equipment. Earnings can go toward better cleaning tools and greater carrying capacity, while cleaning vehicles become available as the operation grows.

That progression gives the repetitive task its purpose: collect enough dust to improve how you work, then use those improvements to tackle more of the Moon. The official description confirms the broad progression, but does not provide a complete tool list or explain when each vehicle unlocks.

## Playing solo or in co-op

*Moon Sweepers* supports single-player and online co-op lobbies of up to 16 players. In co-op, your crew can spread out to clean different areas or work together on the same patch of terrain.

The shared goal remains the same: collect dust, make deliveries, and expand the cleanup operation. The Steam listing does not explain how earnings are divided among players, so don’t assume a particular reward-sharing system.

## Moon Sweepers gameplay FAQ

### What do you do in Moon Sweepers?

You clean lunar terrain, collect dust, deliver it to a depot for payment, and buy equipment upgrades to continue the job.

### Can you play Moon Sweepers alone?

Yes. Steam lists both single-player and online co-op as supported modes.

### How many players can join a co-op lobby?

Online co-op lobbies support up to 16 players, according to the developer’s Steam description.

### Are there vehicles in Moon Sweepers?

Yes. The developer says cleaning vehicles become part of your operation as it grows, but the Steam description does not specify their unlock requirements.
