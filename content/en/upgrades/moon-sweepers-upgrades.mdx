## What Moon Sweepers Upgrades Are and Why They Matter

Moon Sweepers is a co-op cleaning sim by DragDrop Games where you collect lunar dust, sell it, and reinvest the earnings into your crew. Upgrades are the core progression loop: better cleaning tools, suit gear, vehicles, and base buildings all come from the money you make sweeping the Moon's surface.

Here's the key thing to understand before spending a single dollar: **money and upgrades are shared by the whole crew, while tools and vehicles remain personal.** In a co-op session of up to 16 players, every upgrade you buy benefits everyone, so coordinate with your crew before committing funds.

## The Upgrade Matrix: 23 Upgrades Across 5 Branches

The backbone of Moon Sweepers upgrades is the upgrade matrix, which contains 23 upgrades organized into 5 branches. Each upgrade has:

- A cost per level
- A specific effect on your cleaning operation
- Prerequisites you must unlock first
- Capstones at the end of each branch

Because branches have prerequisites and capstones, planning matters. A dedicated upgrade calculator exists on the [Moon Sweepers wiki](https://wikily.gg/moon-sweepers) to help you map out costs before you buy, which is especially useful for crews splitting shared money.

## Cleaning Tools and Containers

There are 6 cleaning tools in the game, running from the humble Toothbrush up to the Extra Wide Broom. Each tool has its own price, dust container size, and cleaning width, and tools support their own container upgrades to increase how much dust you can carry before dumping.

Wider cleaning width and bigger containers mean fewer trips back to sell, so tool upgrades directly boost your income rate — dust sells at $1 per kg, sand at $3, and moon stone at $7.

## Suit Gear: 5 Personal Upgrades

Suit gear covers the 5 personal upgrades your astronaut wears:

- **Tool Belt**
- **Forward Jetpack**
- **Gravity Boots**
- **Helmet Lamp**
- **Night Vision Goggles**

Each suit gear item has multiple levels with a price and effect per level. The Helmet Lamp and Night Vision Goggles are particularly relevant because every location on the Moon gets 30 minutes of night in each 60-minute day/night cycle — gear that helps you work in the dark keeps your crew productive around the clock.

## Vehicles and Their Upgrades

There are 5 vehicles, from the Push Sweeper up to the Crew Transport. Each has a garage price, cleaning width, hopper size, seat count, and its own upgrade costs. Vehicles pair cleaning capacity with team mobility, and the Crew Transport's seats matter when moving a large co-op crew across the Moon's 26 lunar sites.

Beyond cleaning, vehicle tricks pay out on landing, so upgraded vehicles can earn money even when you're not sweeping.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do upgrades work in Moon Sweepers co-op?**
Money and upgrades are shared by the entire crew (up to 16 players), while tools and vehicles are owned individually by each player.

**How many upgrades are in the Moon Sweepers upgrade matrix?**
The upgrade matrix contains 23 upgrades spread across 5 branches, each with per-level costs, prerequisites, and capstones.

**What are the suit gear upgrades in Moon Sweepers?**
There are 5 suit gear upgrades: Tool Belt, Forward Jetpack, Gravity Boots, Helmet Lamp, and Night Vision Goggles, each with multiple price-and-effect levels.

**How do you earn money to buy upgrades?**
Sell dust ($1/kg), sand ($3/kg), and moon stone ($7/kg), plus bonuses for cleared regions ($10), untangled flags ($100), surveyed lunar sites ($250), and vehicle tricks on landing.
