## Start with the dust-to-delivery loop

A toothbrush is a slow start when you have a Moon to clean. The most useful Moon Sweepers tip is to keep the full loop in mind: collect lunar dust, fill your storage, deliver it to a depot, launch a shipment, and spend the earnings on better equipment. That matters because cleaning alone does not pay for your next upgrade; shipments do.

The developer’s [Moon Sweepers Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5335510/Moon_Sweepers/) confirms that sequence, but does not specify an ideal shipment size or a fastest route. As you play, pay attention to how often you need to stop cleaning to make a delivery.

## Choose upgrades around what slows you down

Earnings can go toward better cleaning tools and increased carrying capacity. Rather than treating either as an automatic first purchase, notice which part of the loop feels limiting:

- If gathering dust takes most of your time, look at the available cleaning-tool upgrades.
- If storage fills before you can finish working an area, consider more carrying capacity.

The official description does not provide upgrade costs or efficiency comparisons, so there is no verified universal purchase order.

## Keep depots close to the work

Moon Sweepers lets you place depots near the areas you are cleaning. Use that option as the operation spreads: a depot near your current work gives you a convenient destination for dust deliveries and shipments.

The game also lets you track cleaning progress across the lunar surface. Check that progress when choosing where to work next, especially if it is becoming hard to tell which terrain your crew has already covered. Cleaning vehicles become available as the operation grows, but the source does not establish when to buy them or how they compare with other upgrades.

## Give your co-op crew a plan

Online co-op supports up to 16 players. The official description says crews can spread out to cover more ground or work together on the same area, so agree on an approach before everyone starts sweeping. If you spread out, make sure players know which area they are tackling and where to deliver their dust.

Neither approach is confirmed to be faster in every situation. The practical goal is to avoid having a large crew unsure where to clean or how to turn collected dust into earnings.

## Moon Sweepers FAQ

### What are the best Moon Sweepers tips and tricks for beginners?

Learn the complete collection-and-shipment loop first. Clean dust, fill storage, deliver it to a depot, launch a shipment, and then use the earnings to address whichever task is slowing you down.

### How do you earn money in Moon Sweepers?

According to the developer’s description, you collect lunar dust, deliver it to a depot, and launch shipments to get paid.

### Should I upgrade my tools or carrying capacity first?

There is no verified best order. Choose based on whether cleaning speed or frequent storage trips are the bigger obstacle in your current session.
